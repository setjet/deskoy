# Deskoy Pro

Deskoy Pro is activated inside the app with a licence key sent to the email address used at checkout. Open Deskoy, go to its licence settings, and enter your key.

For purchase, delivery, or activation help, contact [Deskoy support](https://www.deskoy.com/docs/support). Do not share a licence key in a public issue. Deskoy does not require an app account to activate a licence.
