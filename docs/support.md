# Support

For account, payment, licence, or private diagnostic questions, use [Deskoy support](https://www.deskoy.com/docs/support).

For reproducible product bugs or feature ideas, use [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/setjet/deskoy/issues). Describe what happened, what you expected, and your Deskoy and Windows versions. Remove licence keys, customer details, private screenshots, and credentials before posting.
